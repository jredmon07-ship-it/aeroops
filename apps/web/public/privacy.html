Privacy.

What the public demo stores, and what it does not.

The project

AeroOps is open-source software. There is no hosted paid service. If you self-host AeroOps, your copy and its data are yours to run, and this page does not cover it.

The public demo

The demo at aeroops-web.vercel.app holds fictional sample data. It stores only what visitors type or upload into the demo organizations (such as a profile photo), along with the sign-in and audit records the app keeps for them; the hosting providers (Vercel and Neon) may keep standard request logs. Anything you enter is visible to other visitors and may be wiped at any time. Please do not enter real personal information.

This page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log standard request data.

Payments

AeroOps does not process payments. The demo does not collect card or bank details.

Tracking

The demo shows no ads, does not sell data, and does not track you across other sites.

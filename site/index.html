The product

Built around the four jobs that actually run an operation.

Most scheduling tools stop at the calendar. AeroOps follows the aircraft through its whole day: booked, dispatched, squawked, repaired, invoiced.

01 · Scheduling and dispatch

A schedule that refuses to double-book.

Every reservation, recurring series, reschedule, and auto-scheduled slot passes through one gate. If it conflicts, it never lands on the board.

  • Conflict detection on a single shared create path, so there is no back door that skips it.
  • Booking policies you define: maximum duration, advance limits, lead time, blackout windows.
  • Recurring reservations that expand correctly across daylight-saving changes.
  • A drag-and-drop dispatch board with check-out, check-in, and write-once Hobbs and tach entries.
  • Civil-twilight awareness: flag or block reservations that run past dusk, under rules you set.

02 · Maintenance

Maintenance that actually grounds aircraft.

Most tools send maintenance reminders. AeroOps enforces the decision: when your operation grounds an aircraft, nobody can dispatch it until the work order that grounded it is closed.

  • Squawks tracked from report to resolution, with severity your team assigns.
  • Operator grounding as an append-only record: who, when, why.
  • A hard dispatch gate. Grounded means grounded until the work is done.
  • Work orders with return to service: complete the order, resolve the squawk, and put the tail back on the line in one step.
  • Sign-offs are write-once. Records are corrected by new entries, never silent edits.

03 · Training records

Training records that keep their integrity as you grow.

Courses, ordered lessons, enrollments, and instructor lesson records built for the record-keeping discipline a growing school needs, whether you run a two-CFI club today or a structured Part 141 program later.

  • Your syllabus, your structure: operator-defined courses and ordered lessons.
  • Write-once lesson records. A signed record is never edited; corrections are new, attributed entries.
  • Progress at a glance for every enrollment, drawn from actual lesson coverage.
  • An audit trail on everything: records integrity is the default, not a plan tier.

04 · Billing

From Hobbs to invoice without a spreadsheet.

The meters your dispatch tickets already capture become the invoice. No retyping, no end-of-month reconstruction from a whiteboard.

  • Rate schedules per aircraft, wet or dry, plus instructor time.
  • Draft invoices generated from closed dispatch tickets, reviewed by a human before anything goes out.
  • Exact math: every line computed in integer cents and property-tested.
  • Built to hand clean numbers to QuickBooks Online, so your books and your flight line agree.

Pricing philosophy

Priced per aircraft. Never per seat.

Per-seat pricing punishes a flight school for the exact thing it exists to do: add students. AeroOps charges for tails on the line, so your tenth instructor and your two-hundredth student cost the same as your first.

Get in touch

Card processors take a percentage of every rental and lesson you collect: a $3,000 block-time purchase costs about $87 in card fees. Route the same payment through AeroOps by bank transfer and it costs $7, the cap, no matter how large the payment gets. Bill monthly statements and the cap works even harder: one capped payment per member, so your effective rate falls as your members fly more. Cards are still accepted for the occasional payment, at processor cost passed through at checkout; the money that matters rides the bank-transfer rail instead.

A flight is scheduled, dispatched, and closed out with a recorded Hobbs reading; that reading pre-fills a draft invoice for a person to review and finalize. Turn on auto-pay for a member and each finalized invoice collects from their linked bank account automatically. The QuickBooks Online export splits rental and instruction into their own accounts on the way out.

Most flight-school software bills you per aircraft, every month, a tax on growing your fleet. AeroOps charges nothing per aircraft, ever.

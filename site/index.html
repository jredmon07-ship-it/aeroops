Most scheduling tools stop at the calendar. AeroOps follows the aircraft through the whole day: booking, dispatch, maintenance, and training. One system, one source of truth, no spreadsheets in between.

For flying clubs

Built for flying clubs.

AeroOps starts with the club: a shared fleet, volunteer officers, and members who fly on their own time.

One shared calendar

One schedule for every tail that can’t double-book, with per-airplane checkout rules.

Officer roles and handoff

Member roles and a portal, so next year’s board inherits a login. Club mode hides what a small club doesn’t need.

The platform

Four hubs, one system.

Everything a flying club, flight school, or FBO runs on: scheduling, training, maintenance, and reporting in one place.

IScheduling

A schedule that refuses to double-book.

Every reservation, recurring series, and reschedule passes through one conflict check.

  • Drag-and-drop dispatch board
  • Check-out and check-in with write-once Hobbs and tach
  • Booking policies you set: duration, advance limits, lead time, blackout windows
  • Live METAR, Zulu clock, and civil twilight on every screen
/o/alpha/schedule
IITraining

Write-once training records.

Instructors write lesson records that lock the moment they are entered.

  • Load your own syllabus, lesson by lesson
  • Fits both Part 61 and Part 141 training programs
  • Corrections append, never overwrite
  • Progress drawn from actual lesson coverage
/o/alpha/training/private-pilot
IIIMaintenance

Maintenance that actually grounds aircraft.

A grounded aircraft cannot be dispatched until the grounding is resolved.

  • Aircraft usage from the meters dispatch already records
  • Work orders and squawks, from report to resolution
  • Write-once sign-offs, never silently edited
  • Full audit trail on every record
/o/alpha/maintenance/squawks
IVReporting hub

See the whole operation at a glance.

Right now and the last thirty days, on one screen.

  • Flights, hours, and utilization per tail
  • Reservations vs. flights and cancellations
  • Grounded aircraft, open squawks, and today's flights, live
  • Every report scoped to any date range, exportable to CSV
/o/alpha/reporting/reports

Open source

Open source.

AeroOps is free and open source under the MIT license. You can read the code and run it yourself.

  • Next.js and PostgreSQL with row-level security
  • Self-host on Vercel and Neon, or any Postgres
  • Setup steps in the README

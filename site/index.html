Pricing

Free.

Scheduling, dispatch, maintenance, training records, billing, reporting, a public booking page, and a member portal: no subscription, no per-aircraft fee, no per-seat fee. If you collect payments through AeroOps, bank transfers are the default rail at 1%, capped at $10 per transaction, so a $5,000 block purchase costs $10 to collect, never $50 or $125. Founding operators keep a $5 cap forever. Cards are accepted too, passed through at processor cost (2.9% plus 30 cents) with no markup. Beyond that, there is nothing else on the price list.

Assumptions: average payment $350, collected by bank transfer where supported, using published pricing of leading alternatives verified July 2026. AeroOps: $0 software; bank transfers 1% capped at $10 per payment (this page's published pricing). Typical per-aircraft platform: $10 per aircraft per month, plus $14.95 per month in processing and a 2.5% blended card rate, the midpoint of a published 1.09%-2.9% range (no bank-transfer rate is published). Typical subscription platform: pricing is quote-only, estimated $60-$250 per month in software by fleet size, plus a published 1% ACH rate capped at $100 per transaction. Your actual quotes may differ; that is rather the point.

Card processors take a percentage of every rental and lesson you collect: a $3,000 block-time purchase costs about $87 in card fees. Route the same payment through AeroOps by bank transfer and it costs $10, the cap, no matter how large the payment gets. Bill monthly statements and the cap works even harder: one capped payment per member, so your effective rate falls as your members fly more. Cards are still accepted for the occasional payment, at processor cost passed through at checkout; the money that matters rides the bank-transfer rail instead.

A flight is scheduled, dispatched, and closed out with a recorded Hobbs reading; that reading pre-fills a draft invoice for a person to review and finalize. Turn on auto-pay for a member and each finalized invoice collects from their linked bank account automatically. The QuickBooks Online export splits rental and instruction into their own accounts on the way out.

Most flight-school software bills you per aircraft, every month, a tax on growing your fleet. AeroOps charges nothing per aircraft, ever.

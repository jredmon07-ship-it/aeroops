Free scheduling, dispatch, maintenance, training records, and billing for flight schools, flying clubs, and FBOs. No per-aircraft fee, no per-instructor fee, no subscription. Unlimited instructors, students, and staff.

The product

Four jobs, one system.

Most scheduling tools stop at the calendar. AeroOps follows the aircraft through the day it books, dispatches, and bills for.

  1. I

    A schedule that refuses to double-book.

    Every reservation, recurring series, reschedule, and auto-scheduled slot passes through one conflict gate, so there is no way around it.

    • Booking policies you define: maximum duration, advance limits, lead time, blackout windows
    • A drag-and-drop dispatch board with check-out, check-in, and write-once Hobbs and tach entries
    • Civil-twilight aware, flagged or blocked under rules your operation sets
  2. II

    Maintenance that actually grounds aircraft.

    When your team grounds an aircraft, nobody can dispatch it until the work order that grounded it is closed.

    • Squawks tracked from report to resolution, severity your team assigns
    • A hard dispatch gate: grounded means grounded until the work is done
    • Work orders with return to service, and write-once sign-offs that are never silently edited
  3. III

    Training records that hold their integrity as you grow.

    Build your own courses and ordered lessons, enroll members, and let instructors write lesson records that lock the moment they are entered.

    • Operator-defined courses and ordered lessons, your syllabus and your structure
    • Write-once lesson records; corrections append as new entries, never overwrite
    • Progress at a glance, drawn from actual lesson coverage
  4. IV

    From Hobbs to invoice without a spreadsheet.

    The meters your dispatch tickets already capture become the draft invoice, calculated in exact cents and reviewed by a person before anything goes out.

    • Rate schedules per aircraft, wet or dry, plus instructor time
    • ACH payment with transfer fees capped at $5, never a percentage of the invoice
    • Clean line-item export to QuickBooks Online

Assumptions: average payment $350, collected by bank transfer where supported. AeroOps: $0 software; bank transfers a flat $5 per payment, capped (this page's published pricing). Flight Circle: $10 per aircraft/mo plus $14.95/mo processing and a 2.5% blended card rate — the midpoint of their published “1.09%–2.9%” range (flightcircle.com pricing docs; they do not publish a bank-transfer rate). Flight Schedule Pro: pricing is quote-only — estimated $60–$250/mo software by fleet size, plus their published 1% ACH (subscriber agreement). Your actual quotes may differ; that is rather the point.

Card processors take a percentage of every rental and lesson you collect: a $3,000 block-time purchase costs about $87 in card fees. Route the same payment through AeroOps by ACH and it costs $5, flat, capped, regardless of the amount. Cards are still welcome for the occasional payment; the money that matters rides that rail instead.

The loop is built so every lesson collects itself: a flight is scheduled, dispatched, and closed out with a recorded Hobbs reading; that reading becomes the invoice; the invoice debits the student's linked bank account. Nobody re-keys a number. The QuickBooks Online export splits rental and instruction into their own accounts on the way out.

Most flight-school software bills you per aircraft, every month. That is a tax on growing your fleet. AeroOps never does.

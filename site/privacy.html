Legal

Privacy Policy.

What AeroOps collects, why, and who else touches it.

Draft, not yet in force. This document is a working draft published for review. It has not been reviewed by an attorney. A final version will carry an effective date.

What we collect

Account information: your name, email address, phone number, role, and an optional profile photo. Operational records your organization enters: schedules, dispatch and meter readings, maintenance entries, training records, invoices, and payment activity. Technical logs: sign-ins and the actions taken in the product, kept as an audit trail for your organization. Card numbers and bank account details never touch AeroOps servers; Stripe collects and stores those directly.

How we use it

To provide the service to your organization: running the schedule, producing invoices, collecting payments you initiate, and showing your organization its own records. We do not sell personal information, we do not run advertising, and we do not use your records to train anything or market to your members.

Who else touches it

Service providers under contract: Stripe (payment processing), our hosting and database providers (application and data hosting), an email delivery provider (notifications you ask for), and Intuit QuickBooks Online, only if your organization connects it, and only the invoice and payment records your organization exports. Each receives the minimum needed to do its job.

Your choices

You can review and correct your account information inside the product. Members can ask their organization, or us directly, to review, correct, or delete their personal information; write to the address below and we will respond within thirty days. Organizations can export their data at any time.

Do Not Track

AeroOps does not track visitors across other sites and shows no ads, so there is nothing for a Do Not Track signal to turn off. Browsers sending it are treated like every other browser: untracked.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will give notice inside the product or by email before the change takes effect, and this page will always carry the current version and its effective date.

Getting started

Add your aircraft and people, and you're scheduling.

The first two steps below are the whole requirement, a few minutes each. Everything after that, including billing, is optional and turns on whenever you're ready.

Steps

  1. 1

    Sign in to your workspace Essential

    Every operation gets its own address and its own sign-in page. In the live demo, open aeroops-web.vercel.app and log in as owner@alpha.aero / demo1234. You land on your dashboard: today's flights, any grounded aircraft or open squawks, and your recent utilization, at a glance.

  2. 2

    Add your aircraft Essential

    In Schedule → Resources, add each tail number: a display name and the N-number is all it takes to start scheduling it. From there, open the aircraft's page under Fleet to fill in make, model, year, serial number, and home base, and to say whether it tracks Hobbs, tach, or both.

  3. 3

    Add your people Essential

    In People, add each student, instructor, dispatcher, or staff member: name, email, phone, and role. Moving from another scheduler, upload a roster export as a CSV in Settings → Import instead; you review a preview of what it detected before anything is created.

  4. 4

    Turn on day/night-aware scheduling Optional

    Once your operation's home airport is on file, open Gate settings on the dispatch board and switch on the civil-twilight check, warn or block. From then on, every new reservation shows a day/night indicator automatically. This is a scheduling convenience calculated from astronomy, never an FAA night-currency determination.

  5. 5

    Schedule and dispatch Essential

    Book a reservation against the tail and the instructor; the conflict check and your booking policies hold automatically. When the aircraft flies, check it out on the dispatch board and record Hobbs (and tach, if it tracks one), then check it back in the same way when it returns.

    Something needs attention: log a squawk at check-in. An aircraft grounded for a squawk cannot be dispatched again until the grounding is resolved.

  6. 6

    Billing, payments, and QuickBooks Optional

    Turn billing on when you're ready. We set your rate schedule up with you, per aircraft and for instruction; from there, every closed dispatch produces a draft invoice from the recorded Hobbs time. Review it in Billing, finalize it, and collect payment by bank transfer for a flat, capped fee, cards welcome too, or record a payment you took another way.

    Connect QuickBooks Online once, in Settings, and export a clean line-item file, split between rental and instruction accounts, whenever you close your books.
